Supplement StackGoals

What we optimize for and why it's on the card

Heart health
LDL-C was 128 mg/dL at the March 2026 physical (sample value). Fish oil, soluble fiber and aged garlic support it alongside diet and Zone 2 cardio. Recheck the lipid panel in January before adding anything new.
Sleep
Averaging 6 h 40 min on the tracker, target 7 h 15 min. Evening magnesium and the wind-down powder are the anchors; caffeine cutoff moved to noon in August.
Recovery
Four strength sessions and two long rides per week. Whey and creatine after training, tart cherry on hard days. Knee soreness after long rides is the thing to watch.
Keep it simple
Fewer products, same coverage. The goal for 2027 is under 12 daily items and under $150 a month without losing anything that matters.
