The Cardiometabolic Connection
Why the liver, the lipids and the glucose numbers moved together
In January 2025 Sam's markers told one connected story: high triglycerides (204), low HDL (39), small dense LDL (Pattern B), borderline HbA1c (5.9%), elevated fasting insulin (16.2) and a raised ALT (58). That cluster is the signature of early insulin resistance, where the liver stores extra fat and exports more triglyceride-rich particles.

As waist size, alcohol and refined carbohydrates came down and training went up, the whole cluster improved at once. Triglycerides fell 40%, HDL rose 10 points, LDL shifted to Pattern A and ALT normalized. That is why the TG/HDL ratio (5.2 to 2.5) is one of the most useful single numbers on this dashboard.

ApoB moved less because it counts every atherogenic particle, and part of Sam's LDL level is likely inherited. That is the piece where fiber, sterols or medication may still be needed.
One root cause, many readouts. Keep the habits that fixed the metabolic cluster, then aim the remaining effort at ApoB.
