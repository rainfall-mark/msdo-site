How the system becomes a chat app

Every decision traces to a line in design.md or a value in tokens.json. New pieces are flagged at the bottom.

One blue surface, not a stack

Header, stream and composer now sit on a single brand-blue card at 15px inset on the gray-900 page. The section-card language survives, but the app reads as one continuous space rather than a website scrolled.

The logo does the talking

Official mark top left with Powered by Rainfall beneath it, same pairing as the lockup on the site. The app name is gone from the bar because the logo already says it, which buys back the vertical space the headline needs.

Deep blue is now your voice

With the surface at blue-500, user messages go blue-900 with the chamfered bottom-right corner. That is the speech chip straight out of the "Ask The Two Robbies" artwork, where the bubbles are deep blue and gray-900 on brand blue.

The Robbies stay unbubbled, white text at full measure. Two bubbles would cap line length and read as SMS.

Red earns its four appearances

  • The & Friends tab in the mark
  • The send button, matching the site's submit convention
  • Every hover fill on a chip, source or follow-up
  • The waiting dots and the bullet marks

Never as a line, never as text on blue. The headline is plain white now, so red reads as action rather than emphasis.

Composer sits low and stays put

Two lines by default, so a real question fits without the field growing under your thumb. Round red send in the bottom-right corner of the field, 44px. During generation it becomes a stop button in the same slot, so nothing moves.

The disclaimer moved out of the blue onto the page itself, where it belongs as chrome rather than content.

History lives behind the menu

The hamburger opens a deep-blue panel over the app, same 12px geometry as the card beneath it, so it reads as another card rather than a system sheet. Newest first, grouped Today / Yesterday / Previous 7 days / Previous 30 days / Older.

  • A chat is titled by the question that started it, which is the thing people actually remember.
  • Search filters on questions and answers, so "Saliba" finds the thread even when the title doesn't say it.
  • Tapping a chat restores the full thread and lands it at the action icons, same rest position as a fresh answer.
  • Hitting + files the chat on screen into history rather than throwing it away.
  • Esc closes, focus goes to the search box on open and back to the menu button on close.

New pieces, flagged

  • Round icon buttons — the ring appears on hover, inverting the site's Social Link. Plus is new chat, hamburger is the menu.
  • Episode badges — display caps on a block, the pattern design.md points at. Deep-link to episode and timestamp. Main trust lever for factual football answers.
  • Message actions — copy, rate, share. Always visible at 40px. Hover-reveal fails on touch.
  • System banner — offline, rate limit, error, stopped. Named causes with a recovery action.
  • Secondary text at 82% white — the site's 61% muted grey fails contrast on blue-500 at this size. 82% clears 4.5:1.

Still open

  • Countach stands in as Big Shoulders Display. Headline texture is off until the real face is licensed for web.
  • The menu's contents are undecided. History, saved answers and settings are the obvious three.
  • Share is drawn but not designed. A branded quote card is the growth loop worth building.
  • Desktop is this layout widened, not a different one. Worth confirming before I build it.
